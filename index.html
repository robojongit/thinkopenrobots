Robotics software, open to everyone

We believe that software for robotics should be open for everyone to use. Optimizing models and harnesses shouldn't happen only inside the labs, and we think that with today's coding agents anyone who wants to can contribute. That's why we started this challenge.

Tasks

Seven RoboSuite tasks in simulation, from the CaP-X benchmark (paper, project page).

  • Cube lifting
  • Stacking
  • Restacking
  • Nut assembly
  • Wiping a spill
  • Two-arm lift
  • Two-arm handover
